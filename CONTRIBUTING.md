# Contributing

Reports for another Plasma version must include the exact Plasma, Qt, KF6, and
distribution versions; build output; whether the panel is horizontal or
vertical; and which checklist items were actually tested.

Keep version-specific patches in their own directory. Do not silently claim a
new version as supported because a patch applies: compile the complete applet
and perform the interaction checklist.

Changes derived from KDE MR 6958 must retain clear attribution to its author.
Avoid bundling unrelated panel behavior or replacing the system package.
